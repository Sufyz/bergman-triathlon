Race Day Plan
September 6, 2026 · Bergman Bangalore Olympic
2 Days Before — Sep 4
  • ✓Carb-load: increase rice/chapati by 20–30%. Normal protein, low fat and fibre.
  • ✓Hydrate aggressively + electrolytes (ORS / coconut water / salt+lemon).
  • ✓Short shakeout: 20 min easy swim + 15 min easy ride + 10 min jog.
  • ✓Lay out all gear: tyres, helmet, bib, goggles, run shoes, on-bike nutrition.
Race Eve — Sep 5
  • ✓Big carb dinner by 7 PM: rice + chicken/eggs + vegetables. Nothing unfamiliar.
  • ✓Complete rest day — no training.
  • ✓Set 2 alarms. In bed by 9:30–10 PM.
Race Morning — Sep 6
  • ✓Wake 2.5–3 hrs before start. Familiar breakfast: banana + oats + 2 eggs.
  • ✓Arrive 60–90 min early. Walk T1 and T2 — memorise the layout.
  • ✓10-min warm-up swim to calm nerves and wake up your stroke.
